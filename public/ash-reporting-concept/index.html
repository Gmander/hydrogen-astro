A working collection of reporting ideas for a photography team. See the pace of production, spot the bottlenecks, and know what needs a conversation.

Illustrative mockup. All names, jobs and numbers are invented. No monday.com connection. Metrics are candidates to explore, subject to the fields and history your team records.

01 / The pace of the business

VOLUME

How many shoots are we completing?

Weekly shoot count, with the trend in plain sight.

Count unique shoots with a completed shoot status, grouped by actual shoot date. A completed shoot can still be in editing. Exclude cancellations, test items and duplicate task rows. Requires a reliable shoot identifier, status and date.
WORK MIX

What kind of work fills the calendar?

Completed shoots by service type.

Useful for understanding demand and deciding where to focus capacity.
Same completed-shoot population as the volume chart, grouped by service category. Missing categories should appear as “Unclassified.” Volume does not imply revenue or profitability.
TURNAROUND

How quickly do clients get their photos?

Median calendar days from shoot to delivery, by delivery week.

Track the client experience alongside production volume.
Delivered jobs only, grouped by actual delivery date. Median of actual delivery date minus actual shoot date; not a difference between averages. Requires both dates. This population differs from the completed-shoot count.
RELIABILITY

Are we delivering when we promised?

On-time delivery rate, by delivery week.

Delivered on or before the committed deadline ÷ delivered jobs with a recorded deadline. Preserve the original promise or agree how deadline changes are handled. Exclude and disclose missing deadlines; do not treat them as on-time.

One current stage per unique job. Counts are inventory at a point in time, not stage conversion rates. Overdue means undelivered with a committed delivery date before the snapshot date. A larger stage count alone does not prove a bottleneck; age and staffing add context.
LOOKING AHEAD

Who has shoots coming up?

Booked shoots per photographer · Nov 9–15, 2026.

A starting point for a scheduling conversation. Shoot counts alone do not measure available capacity.
Count confirmed bookings by primary photographer and scheduled shoot date. Sample: Maya 7, Jordan 5, Casey 4, unassigned 2. Duration, travel, editing responsibilities and time off are needed before calling anyone overloaded.
